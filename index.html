    DATA & INTERPRETATION

    What this atlas measures

    Each directed connection counts people with a registered residence in one Swedish municipality and a workplace in another. It is a residence-to-workplace association, not a count of trips, commuting frequency, migration or remote-working days.

    Three statistical series

    • RAMS legacy · TAB333 · 2004-2018: age scope changes in 2011; no employed persons aged 75+ from 2011. Classification also changes.
    • RAMS · TAB5850 · 2019-2021: ages 16-74. The 2019 AGI transition changes the method. Armed Forces reporting changes from 2020.
    • BAS · TAB1830 · 2020-2024: ages 15-74. Entrepreneur classification changes in 2024. Disclosure protection affects small counts; published totals may differ from men + women. Unlocated workplaces are assigned to the residence municipality.

    Series remain separate. Changes across these breaks must not be interpreted as a harmonised time trend. “All persons” uses the independently published total and never adds it to the sex-specific layers.

    Indicators and display

    Residence indicators use a municipality's row in the full domestic matrix; workplace indicators use its column. External share excludes within-municipality links from the numerator and includes them in the denominator. National shares sum published counts first. Internal links are included in indicators but not drawn as curves.

    Mean external distance is weighted by persons and uses great-circle distances between polygon centroids calculated in SWEREF 99 TM (EPSG:3006). It is not a road distance. Diversity is external-link Shannon entropy divided by ln(289). Partner count uses links of at least five persons. Undefined values appear as “—”.

    Colour bins are quintiles across the 290 municipalities in the current layer; their ranges change with the filters. Network curves are schematic, with arrowheads pointing to the workplace. The minimum and connection cap affect drawing only. CSV export contains all external links meeting the threshold in the selected scope, without the display cap. Zero and missing cells are not in the browser link files.

    Geography and attribution

    The map uses Eurostat GISCO LAU 2024, Sweden, generalised at 1:1 million, in WGS84. All years use fixed 2024 boundaries. Matching municipality codes do not establish invariant historical boundaries; no geographic crosswalk has been applied.
